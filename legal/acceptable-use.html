Acceptable Use Policy

1.0.0 · Effective 25 August 2026

Authorised research only: lawful OSINT, journalism, academic research, education, public-interest advocacy using public information.

Prohibited: unauthorised access; credential harvesting; malware; stalking, doxxing, or intimidation; child sexual exploitation material in any form; fraud; private surveillance beyond already-published information; offences under Australian law including Criminal Code Act 1995 (Cth) computer offences.

The desk must not be used to locate, identify, or target a private person for harm. Cite sources. Distinguish allegation from finding. Do not present model text as evidence.

Public sources only. Authorised research. Not a law-enforcement system.
