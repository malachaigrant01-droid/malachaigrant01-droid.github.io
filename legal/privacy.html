Privacy Policy

1.0.0 · Effective 25 August 2026

Written to sit alongside the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Investigation notes live in browser local storage. This build has no user accounts. Server functions receive only the query you submit to call public APIs.

We do not collect TFNs, health records, biometrics, payment cards, or private communications. No advertising fingerprints. No sale of personal information.

Third parties: OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Open-Meteo, USGS, BBC, ABC, Police.uk (England & Wales). Use official police portals to report crime. This desk does not file reports for you.

If you request synthesis, the bounded prompt and public facts go to the xAI API under the app owner’s key. Do not paste other people’s sensitive personal information.

Delete reports in the tree or clear site data. Children: not directed at children under 16. Complaints: OAIC · privacy@malachai.com.

Public sources only. Authorised research. Not a law-enforcement system.
