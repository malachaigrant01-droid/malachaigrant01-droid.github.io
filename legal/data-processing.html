Public-data processing

1.0.0 · Effective 25 August 2026

ABiZmuth processes only information that is already public or that you typed yourself.

Place search: Nominatim. Weather: Open-Meteo. Earthquakes: USGS. News: BBC World and ABC News RSS. Crime: Police.uk street-level open data for England & Wales; elsewhere official reporting links (AFP, Police.uk, USA.gov, RCMP, NZ Police, Interpol notices).

No closed-source brokers. No people-search paid databases. No social-graph scraping.

Public sources only. Authorised research. Not a law-enforcement system.
