Where your money goes

Apple Pay

Not connected

Apple Pay moves card money. It doesn't buy assets. Paying into this page would only send money to a merchant account. You still wouldn't own anything. To fund real investments with Apple Pay from this app, it needs a regulated partner:

  1. A broker or crypto exchange with an embedded-investing API that holds the assets in your name. It must take fractional orders from A$2.
  2. That partner's Apple Pay / card funding, with the merchant domain verified for this site.
  3. In Australia, an AFS licence (or authorised-representative status) to offer this to anyone other than yourself.

Until then: fund your broker with Apple Pay inside the broker's own app. Then use this page to choose assets and send the order list there.
