How it decides

  • Buy when an asset is trending up (12-bar average above 26-bar), momentum is rising (MACD) and RSI is 50–70: strong but not stretched. Strongest signals go first.
  • Take profit at a likely peak when the price is stretched (RSI 70+ or above its 2σ band) and momentum starts fading. This only happens once the position is up by the % you set.
  • Trailing stop: once the position is up by the trailing %, sell if the price falls that % below its highest point since buying.
  • Stop-loss: before that, sell if the price falls the stop-loss % below the buy price.
  • After selling, it waits 60 minutes before buying the same asset again. It only trades assets whose last trade is under 15 minutes old, so it won't trade shares while their market is closed.
  • Signals use completed 5-minute bars. Crypto starts with about 29 hours of history. Shares build history while this page is open during market hours (35 bars is about 3 hours), and need a Finnhub key.
  • In a long rally it can take profit early and miss the rest. Rules like these often do worse than simply holding, especially after fees.
  • It only runs while this page is open. Close the tab or lock your phone and it stops.

    Where your money goes

    Apple Pay

    Not connected

    Apple Pay moves card money. It doesn't buy assets. Paying into this page would only send money to a merchant account. You still wouldn't own anything. To fund real investments with Apple Pay from this app, it needs a regulated partner:

    1. A broker or crypto exchange with an embedded-investing API that holds the assets in your name. It must take fractional orders from A$2.
    2. That partner's Apple Pay / card funding, with the merchant domain verified for this site.
    3. In Australia, an AFS licence (or authorised-representative status) to offer this to anyone other than yourself.

    Until then: fund your broker with Apple Pay inside the broker's own app. Then use this page to choose assets and send the order list there.
